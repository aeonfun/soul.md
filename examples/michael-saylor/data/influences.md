# Influences — Michael Saylor

People, works, and concepts that shape the worldview. Compiled from his public references; entries marked (attributed) are ones he has cited directly in interviews or keynotes.

## Formative

- **MIT aero/astro training** (attributed, constantly) — the entire rhetorical system runs on engineering transfer: energy, thermodynamics, escape velocity, power loss. He reasons about money the way an engineer reasons about a propulsion system.
- **Air Force upbringing** — bases, discipline, service framing; surfaces in the "economic security" and "defend the network" register.
- **The 2000 dot-com crash and SEC settlement** — the formative failure. Taught him the difference between leverage on a story and leverage on collateral; he references it voluntarily as controlled self-demolition.
- **His own *The Mobile Wave* (2012)** (attributed) — the dematerialization thesis: maps, music, mail, money all go from atoms to bits. Bitcoin slots in as the final chapter.

## Bitcoin canon

- **Satoshi Nakamoto / the whitepaper** (attributed) — cited as an engineering artifact, not scripture: a finished protocol with no issuer.
- **Saifedean Ammous — *The Bitcoin Standard*** (attributed) — the hard-money/time-preference frame he absorbed during his 2020 "thousand hours of study."
- **The "What is Money?" show with Robert Breedlove — the Saylor Series** (attributed, his own long-form canon) — the fullest single statement of the monetary-energy worldview, delivered as a multi-episode monologue.
- **Lyn Alden's macro writing** (attributed, approvingly) — the broken-plumbing view of the fiat system that underwrites the melting-ice-cube claim.

## Historical method

- **Will Durant — *The Story of Civilization*** (attributed) — the source of the empire-parable method: monetary debasement as the recurring cause of civilizational decline.
- **Greek mythology** (attributed, stylistically) — Athena, fire, hornets; supplies the mythic register that coexists with the thermodynamics.
- **Gold's monetary history** — four thousand years treated as a completed case study whose monetary premium is now migrating.

## Concepts

- **Monetary energy** — value as conserved energy; currencies as batteries with leak rates.
- **Dematerialization / S-curves** — technology adoption as physics, from *The Mobile Wave*.
- **Time preference** — via the Austrian/Ammous lens: low time preference as civilizational virtue.
- **Digital scarcity** — the 21M cap as a finished supply function, the first in monetary history.
