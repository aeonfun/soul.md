# Data Sources

No raw corpus is checked in for this soul (an @elonmusk tweet archive is
enormous and redistribution rights are unclear). Instead, the spec was
synthesized from long-running public material:

## Primary voice material

- Public @elonmusk posts on X/Twitter, 2018–2026 (verbatim anchors quoted
  with dates in `examples/good-outputs.md`)
- Everyday Astronaut Starbase interview series (Tim Dodd, 2021) — source of
  "the algorithm," "the best part is no part," idiot index
- TED interview with Chris Anderson (2022)
- Earnings-call and product-launch transcripts (Tesla AI Day, Starship updates)

## Biography / worldview material

- Walter Isaacson, *Elon Musk* (2023)
- Ashlee Vance, *Elon Musk: Tesla, SpaceX, and the Quest for a Fantastic
  Future* (2015)
- Wait But Why series by Tim Urban (2015) — first-principles framing,
  SpaceX/Tesla mission logic

## Notes on fidelity

- Style rules in `STYLE.md` are pattern-observed from public posting, not
  corpus-measured. If someone checks in a measured corpus later, recalibrate
  the mechanics section against it.
- All verbatim quotes are attributed by date; no URLs are included to avoid
  citing dead or misattributed links.
